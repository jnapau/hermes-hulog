# Hulog

Hulog is Hermes Academy’s daily financial-literacy game for Filipino learners. A daily drop contains three cards, with Filipino questions and choices and optional English context. Calendar days use Asia/Manila.

Players create a username and password account, learn through scam identification, budgeting and money choices, earn coins, build a forgiving daily streak, and reveal a collection reward after their Sunday drop. Five Aparador shelves cover Philippine money, national symbols and cultural treasures, farm life, mythology costumes, and traditional clothing. Wearables visibly change Hestia on the dashboard.

Preserve the official Hestia flame companion artwork, the bamboo coin bank, local Plus Jakarta Sans fonts, and the existing cream, honey, cocoa and Hermes blue identity. General controls and navigation are English; Filipino cultural names and learning material remain Filipino.

Administrators alone can manage the question bank/calendar, user roles, and learning analytics. Supabase Auth owns credentials. Database policies and validated functions own authorization, scoring, and rewards. Ordinary players cannot write roles, coins, answers, or unlock records directly.
